Longitudinal survival laboratory

SYNTHETIC DATA ONLY. These models were trained on 600 invented people. The generator deliberately makes smaller hippocampal volume associated with earlier impairment. This tests the software and provides no evidence about Alzheimer disease or anyone's prognosis.

The model retains the interval between the last unimpaired assessment and first positive assessment. People without an observed event remain right-censored at their last assessment. It compares age + cognition with age + cognition + normalized hippocampal volume.

Held-out software experiment

Observation log likelihood measures the probability assigned to the observed event intervals and censoring. Higher is better. It is not percent accuracy. The paired bootstrap interval conditions on these fitted models and does not include training uncertainty. No exact onset times or unknown three-year labels were invented for evaluation.
