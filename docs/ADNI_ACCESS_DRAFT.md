## Proposed-use text to review and adapt

NeuroVolume is a noncommercial educational computational-neuroscience project investigating whether baseline MRI-derived regional brain measurements improve prediction of later recorded cognitive impairment beyond age and baseline cognitive assessments. I am a high-school student attending TAMS. I propose to analyze de-identified longitudinal clinical assessments and processed structural MRI measurements, initially focusing on bilateral hippocampal and intracranial volumes. The work will use participant-separated validation, careful baseline/date alignment, and methods that account for differing follow-up and uncertainty in event observation times. I intend to publish reproducible original code and permitted aggregate research findings, without redistributing participant-level data. Codex has been used to develop the software and study protocol; any use of AI tools with controlled data will be reviewed against applicable access terms and institutional requirements before processing. The project is educational research and will not be used for individual medical diagnosis or treatment.
